# Humaira · Two-minute presentation notes

**Main question:** How are subscription plan and monthly charges associated with cancellation?

### 0:00–0:20 — Introduction
“Our group's wider question is who is more likely to cancel a subscription. My part focuses on two possible factors: the subscription plan and monthly charges. We have 243,787 customer records, and 18.12% are marked as cancelled.”

### 0:20–0:55 — Chart 1: comparing plans
“The first chart is a bar chart. This is appropriate because Basic, Standard and Premium are categories. Basic has the highest cancellation rate at **19.65%**, Standard is **18.43%**, and Premium is **16.28%**. The figures are percentages within each plan, not raw counts.”

### 0:55–1:30 — Chart 2: monthly charges
“The second chart looks at **five ordered charge bands**. Across all customers, cancellation rises from **13.00%** in the lowest band to **23.80%** in the highest. When I select Basic, Standard or Premium, the line updates to show the relationship within that plan. I can also overlay all three lines.”

### 1:30–1:50 — Why these visuals work
“The bar chart starts at zero and gives exact percentage labels. The line chart orders the charge bands from low to high. We also provide customer counts in tooltips and a table, so the audience can judge the size of each group.”

### 1:50–2:00 — Limitation
“These are observed associations, not proof that pricing causes cancellations. The dataset doesn't specify a currency, so I have not assumed one.”

## If questioned by the lecturer

**How do you calculate a percentage?** Cancelled customers in the selected group divided by all customers in that same group, multiplied by 100.

**What does Churn = 1 mean?** Cancellation (given in the work plan).

**Why not use raw cancellation counts?** Plan and band groups may differ in size; rates are more comparable.

**Does the line chart show change over time?** No. The x-axis is ordered monthly-charge bands, not dates.

**Is the relationship causal?** No. Potential confounders may influence both monthly charges and cancellations.

**What about currency?** Not in the supplied dataset. Confirm externally before adding £ or $.
